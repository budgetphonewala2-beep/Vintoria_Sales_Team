# Compliance — cold outreach for a digital services studio

Not legal advice. This is the working rulebook; when a market's rule is unclear, ask the seller before sending.

## Universal rules (apply everywhere)

1. **Identify yourself.** Real sender name, real company (VINTORIA), a monitored reply address.
2. **One clear opt-out in every cold email**, plain text, no login: "If this isn't relevant, reply 'stop' and I won't
   contact you again."
3. **Honour opt-outs immediately and permanently.** Add the address and the domain to
   `workspace/pipeline/suppressions.md`; never contact that person again, on any channel.
4. **No purchased or scraped contact lists.** Contacts must come from the prospect's own site, their public post, a
   public professional profile, or a lawful database the seller is licensed to use.
5. **No deceptive subject lines, fake replies, fake forwarding, or fake urgency.**
6. **No automated sending without explicit human approval of the exact text and recipient.**
7. **No messaging on WhatsApp or social DMs unless the person is publicly reachable for business contact and the
   seller has approved that channel.**
8. **Keep a record**: date, channel, recipient, exact text, basis for contact, opt-out status.

## Market rules to apply

| Market | Regime | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| United States | CAN-SPAM | Accurate headers and subject, physical postal address in every commercial email, working opt-out honoured within 10 business days |
| United Kingdom | UK GDPR + PECR | B2B email to a corporate address is permitted with legitimate interest, but identify yourself and offer opt-out; keep the legitimate-interest note |
| EU | GDPR + ePrivacy | Country rules differ; prefer a business address clearly related to their role, state why you are contacting them, offer opt-out, minimise data |
| Canada | CASL | Consent or a documented business relationship; identify yourself and your address; opt-out must work; keep proof of consent/basis |
| Australia | Spam Act | Consent (express or inferred from conspicuous business publication), accurate sender ID, functional unsubscribe |
| New Zealand | UEMA | Consent-based; accurate identification; working unsubscribe |
| India | DPDP Act + IT Rules | Use business contact data only, state purpose, honour erasure/opt-out requests, do not process personal data beyond the purpose |

## Channel notes

- **Email** — the only channel with full automation. Cold email is 1-to-1, never a bulk blast; keep daily volume
  modest and consistent; stop the sequence on any reply.
- **LinkedIn** — connection notes and messages are sent manually. Do not automate, do not scrape profiles at scale.
- **WhatsApp** — click-to-chat only; the seller sends. No bulk messaging, no groups without invitation.
- **Calls** — only after a reply, a referral, or a public business number used for the stated purpose.

## Data hygiene

- Store the minimum: name, role, business email, company, source URL, reason for contact.
- No sensitive categories (health, religion, politics, sexual orientation, financial hardship).
- On request: delete the record and confirm in writing.
- Keep suppression data forever; delete the rest after 24 months of inactivity unless the seller says otherwise.

## When to stop and ask the seller

- The prospect is in a regulated industry (health, finance, legal, children).
- The contact is a personal address, not a business one.
- The market is not in the table above.
- The seller wants to email a list they did not build themselves.
