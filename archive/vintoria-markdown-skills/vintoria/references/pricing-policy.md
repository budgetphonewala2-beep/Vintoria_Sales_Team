# Pricing policy — the catalog has no prices

`VINTORIA_270_Services_SERVICE_ONLY_2026.pdf` is deliberately service-only: no rates, packages or commercial terms.
Agents must never fill that gap with a guess.

## Hard rules

1. **Never quote a number** for a service, project, retainer, hour, discount or timeline unless it is written
   verbatim in `workspace/profile/SELLER-PROFILE.md` under `pricing`.
2. **Never invent a package.** "Starter / Growth / Enterprise" tiers may be used as *options* with different scope;
   their prices stay `[PRICE TO CONFIRM]` until the seller fills them.
3. **Never anchor with market rates.** "Agencies usually charge…" is an invented claim.
4. **Never promise a timeline.** Durations may be expressed as phases and dependencies. Dates require the seller.
5. **Never discount to win.** If price is the objection, change scope, not price.

## What an agent may say about money

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| Buyer asks "how much?" | "Depends on scope. I'll send a written scope with options and prices — what's your target launch date?" |
| Buyer asks for a range | "I don't want to guess and mislead you. Two questions and I can give you a real number." |
| Proposal | Print `[PRICE TO CONFIRM]` or `[INVESTMENT: seller to fill]` per option |
| Seller gave a price in the profile | Use exactly that price, with its currency and unit |
| Buyer pushes for a discount | Reduce scope (name the catalog services removed), shorten the phase, or move to a smaller first phase |

## Where prices live

```
workspace/profile/SELLER-PROFILE.md
  ## Pricing
  - Currency: USD
  - Model: fixed-scope project / monthly retainer / hybrid
  - Deal size: [min] – [typical]   (only if the seller confirms)
  - Notes: [what is included, what is extra]
```

If that section is empty, every price-related output uses placeholders and says so in one line to the seller.

## Proposal commercial section — allowed shape

```
Option A — Core scope:      [PRICE TO CONFIRM]
Option B — Recommended:     [PRICE TO CONFIRM]
Option C — Expanded:        [PRICE TO CONFIRM]
Payment terms:              [TO CONFIRM]
Validity:                   14 days from issue (adjust only if the seller agrees)
```

Nothing else. No "value of X", no ROI promise, no "you'll make it back in 30 days".
