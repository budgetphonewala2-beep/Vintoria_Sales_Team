# Cold Outreach Sequence

You write outreach a busy owner actually answers: short, specific, true, and easy to say yes to.

## Before you start

1. **Scripts.** `<SCRIPTS>` is the `scripts/` folder of the `sales` skill (`../sales/scripts/` next to this skill's
   folder). Run them with `python` (Windows) or `python3` (macOS/Linux). Each prints one JSON object — treat it as
   evidence to check, not as truth.
2. **The seller.** Run `python <SCRIPTS>/seller_profile.py check`. This skill builds its queries, emails or prices
   from `SELLER-PROFILE.json`, so if it isn't `Ready.` run the `sales-setup` interview first (when the user is in a
   hurry, ask just the missing required questions in one message and save the answers with `seller_profile.py set`).
3. **The prospect.** Everything about one prospect lives in `prospects/<slug>/` — slug = their domain (`acme.com`), or
   `name@platform` (`anita-desai@linkedin`) for a lead without a website. `python <SCRIPTS>/pipeline.py find <email|name|domain>`
   finds it. Never use one prospect's facts for another.
4. **The pipeline.** `~/.ai-sales-team/pipeline.db` is shared by every agent and session. Before you finish, write
   what you learned (stage, note, next action, attached file) — the next agent only sees what the pipeline shows.
5. **Web access.** Use your agent's own fetch/search first; otherwise `python <SCRIPTS>/fetch_page.py <url> ...` and
   `python <SCRIPTS>/web_search.py "<query>"`; with `APIFY_TOKEN`, `python <SCRIPTS>/lead_sources.py`. If you can't fetch
   something, write "Not found".
6. **Facts only.** Every fact you write must trace to a script output, a page saved with `fetch_page.py --save`, the
   prospect's own words or the seller's profile — never to memory or to a document you wrote yourself. Never invent
   people, titles, emails, connections, results, prices or metrics: one invented detail in a cold email destroys trust
   and can break consumer-protection law. Unknown = "Not found"; a guessed email = `pattern-guessed — verify before
   sending`; missing proof = a visible `[ADD REAL CUSTOMER RESULT]`. Never say something was sent, booked, verified or
   scanned unless a script's output says so. The guards: `../sales/references/truth-rules.md`.

## Steps

1. **Evidence first.** If `prospects/<slug>/DOSSIER.json` is missing, run the deep scan:
   `python <SCRIPTS>/prospect_scan.py <url> [--post-text "<their post>" --post-url <url>]`. Read `DOSSIER.md` (ranked hooks, decision maker,
   best email), other `prospects/<slug>/` files and `python <SCRIPTS>/pipeline.py show <slug>`. Recipient must have a `verified` email,
   or say clearly that it isn't.
   **Channel by source:** they posted asking (LinkedIn/Reddit/HN/X/FB) → reply there first (user posts it, 2 lines,
   helpful, no pitch) + email if verified; Google Maps lead with a phone and no email → WhatsApp click-to-chat;
   Product Hunt launch → email the maker or comment on the launch (user).
2. **First email** — follow `references/first-email-guide.md`: plain text, under 120 words, mentions their post
   or business naturally, ONE specific observation backed by evidence, one soft question as the CTA ("worth a
   quick call?"), the user's signature with postal address and an opt-out line. **No booking link** — the goal of
   the cold emails is a reply; the calendar link is sent only once they say yes (`sales-reply` / `sales-book`).
3. **Sequence** — 4 follow-ups on **day 3, 7, 14 and 28** after email 1 (`python <SCRIPTS>/outreach.py cadence`), each adding something
   new, using `references/playbook.md` and the templates `references/template-outreach-cold.md`, `-warm.md`, `-referral.md`:
   | Email | Day | Content | Words |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 0 | Their post / top hook + one fact + soft question | ≤ 120 |
   | 2 | 3 | Second hook (another audit fact) + a 1-line fix idea | ≤ 70 |
   | 3 | 7 | Proof: a relevant real result, or a mini teardown of their page (`[ADD REAL CUSTOMER RESULT]` if none) | ≤ 90 |
   | 4 | 14 | Different angle: competitor/market fact (Maps comparison, reviews) or a simple question | ≤ 60 |
   | 5 | 28 | Polite breakup: "close the file?" + leave the door open | ≤ 50 |
   Subject lines: 2-5 words, lower-case, specific ("acme homepage on mobile"), no clickbait; follow-ups reply
   in the same thread. Write in plain international English — US spelling for US/CA prospects, UK spelling for
   UK/IE/AU/NZ — no Indian-English idioms ("do the needful", "revert", "kindly"), prices in their currency.
   Pull the signature, offer line and proof only from `SELLER-PROFILE.json` (`python <SCRIPTS>/seller_profile.py show`).
   Use the warm or referral template only for a real, confirmed connection.
4. **Write the files.** `prospects/<slug>/SEQUENCE.json` is the source of truth for the emails
   (`[{"step": 1, "subject": "...", "subject_b": "...", "hook": "<dossier hook key>", "body": "..."}, {"step": 2, "body": "..."}, ...]`;
   follow-ups without a subject reply in the same thread). `subject_b` runs an A/B test (every second prospect gets it) and
   `hook` names the dossier hook email 1 is built on (`their_post`, `pagespeed_score`, `viewport` ...) — both feed `learn.py`. `prospects/<slug>/OUTREACH-SEQUENCE.md` is the human view (`references/output-format.md`)
   with the same emails plus A/B subjects and the LinkedIn touches.
5. **Check all five at once:** `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach.py approve <slug> --to <email> --file prospects/<slug>/SEQUENCE.json --check` runs the
   claim check, the quality score (`python <SCRIPTS>/email_lint.py`, 0-100, must reach `LINT_MIN_SCORE` — aim for 80+),
   opt-out, address, booking-link, over-contact and recipient gates on every email and queues nothing.
   Before that, do the panel review and the deletion test in `references/quality.md`. Fix and re-run
   until it says the check passed (dossier, audit, research and the profile's proof are used as evidence automatically;
   add `--evidence-text "<their post>"` if the post isn't in the dossier).
6. **Show the user and deliver** in the mode `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach.py doctor` reports: `autopilot` → after approval run the same
   approve command without `--check` (each email then goes out on its day in the prospect's morning and the rest
   stops the moment they reply, opt out, bounce or book); `send-one-by-one` / `manual` → see "Before anything is sent".
7. Record: `python <SCRIPTS>/pipeline.py attach <slug> prospects/<slug>/OUTREACH-SEQUENCE.md --kind outreach`.
8. Other channels: LinkedIn/WhatsApp messages are sent by the user; for WhatsApp give a click-to-chat link
   (`python <SCRIPTS>/outreach.py whatsapp <number with country code> --text-file msg.txt`), then log it with `--channel whatsapp`.

## Before anything is sent

- **Claim check** every message: `python <SCRIPTS>/claim_check.py --draft <file> --prospect <slug> [--evidence-text "<their post>"]`.
  Rewrite or delete each unsupported sentence and fill every `[PLACEHOLDER]`. Drafts never count as evidence;
  claims about the seller are checked against the real proof in the profile; a hedged consequence ("so phone
  visitors may…") is fine, an unproven number is not.
- **Compliance:** follow `references/compliance.md` — opt-out line, postal address (US), consent rules (UK/EU/CA/AU/NZ).
- **Approval:** show the user the exact final text. Nothing goes out without their OK (a sequence is approved once,
  as a whole).
- **Delivery:** `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach.py doctor` tells you the mode — `autopilot` (queue the approved sequence with
  `outreach.py approve`; it sends each email on its day and stops on any reply), `send-one-by-one` (`outreach.py send`,
  dry run first, then `--send`), or `manual` (the user sends; you `outreach.py log` it so follow-ups get scheduled).
  Never say something was sent unless a script returned `"sent": true` or the user told you so.
- **No booking link** (Calendly, Cal.com, `BOOKING_URL`) until they've replied with interest — a calendar link in a
  cold email reads as presumptuous and lowers replies; `outreach.py` refuses it.
- Never post, comment or DM on social platforms yourself — give the user the text (or a WhatsApp click-to-chat link).
