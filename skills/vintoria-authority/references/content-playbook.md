# Content playbook — five post formats that bring Vintoria's buyers

Posts go out from the sender's personal LinkedIn profile (people follow people more than company pages). Every post is
useful on its own, true (`../../vintoria-sales/references/truth-rules.md`), and written for a business owner, not for
other developers.

## Hooks that work (first line, ≤ 12 words)
- A specific mistake: "Most clinic websites lose patients at the booking step."
- A number the owner can see in their own business: "Count how many calls you missed after 7 pm last week."
- A before/after from a real project in `PROOF-BANK.md`: "This bakery took orders only on WhatsApp. Then we changed one thing."
- A direct question: "Would you pay for a website that doesn't take bookings?"
Avoid: "I'm excited to announce", "Agree?", "Here's the thing", "Let that sink in", clickbait that the post doesn't answer.

## The 5 formats

### 1. Niche tip (Monday)
> **Most clinic websites lose patients at the booking step.**
>
> Three things that fix it:
> 1. A "Book" button on every page, visible on a phone without scrolling.
> 2. Real time slots instead of "call us to book".
> 3. A confirmation on WhatsApp, because that's where patients actually look.
>
> None of this needs a big budget. It needs someone to test the booking flow on a phone before launch.
>
> If you run a clinic: how do patients book with you today?

### 2. How we did it (Tuesday — only from `PROOF-BANK.md`, permission = yes)
Problem in the client's words → what we built (one catalog service, plain words) → the result exactly as the client
said it, with "they told us" → one lesson any owner can use. Name the client only with `name yes`.

### 3. Mistake teardown (Wednesday)
A pattern seen across many sites (from audits), never a named business: "We looked at 20 restaurant sites in Pune this
month. 14 had the menu as a PDF that's unreadable on a phone." Only if the count is real and recorded in `HISTORY.md` /
audits; otherwise write it without a number ("A lot of restaurant sites still…").

### 4. Question to the audience (Thursday)
One real question buyers face, 2-3 options, ask for their experience. Every answer is a conversation starter — reply to
each one (`../SKILL.md`, "Turn engagement into leads").

### 5. Behind the scenes / lesson (Friday)
How Vintoria scopes a project, what a first call covers, a mistake we made and fixed, how long a typical build stage takes
(only figures in `SELLER-PROFILE.md`). Honest and specific beats polished.

## 4-week rotation by lane
Week 1 web & booking · week 2 Shopify / e-commerce · week 3 AI chatbots & automation · week 4 apps & MVPs (only lanes
active in `OFFER.md`). Each week's tips come from `../../vintoria-outreach/references/comment-angles.md`.

## Rules
- One post a day at most; never the same post twice; no reposting other people's content as ours.
- No prices unless in the profile, no guaranteed results, no client names or logos without permission.
- Tag a person only with their permission; never tag prospects to get attention.
- Log each post in `CONTENT-LOG.md`: date, format, hook, link, and after 48 h: impressions, reactions, comments, warm leads.
