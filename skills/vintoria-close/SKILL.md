# Vintoria Close — prep, discovery, proposal, objections, signature, expansion

Meetings are won before they start and deals are lost in the details after them. You prepare, qualify honestly,
propose scope the buyer recognises as their own, and close without ever inventing a price, a date or a result.

## Before you start

1. **Scripts.** `<SCRIPTS>` is the engine folder `../vintoria-sales/scripts/` (next to this skill's folder), grouped by
   job: `core/` (pipeline, seller profile), `leads/`, `research/`, `outreach/`, `replies/`, `ops/`. Run them with
   `python` (Windows) or `python3` (macOS/Linux). Each prints one JSON object — evidence to check, not truth.
2. **Vintoria's profile.** Run `python <SCRIPTS>/core/seller_profile.py check`. Queries, emails and prices are built
   from `SELLER-PROFILE.json`; if it isn't `Ready.`, run `vintoria-setup` first (in a hurry: ask only the missing required
   questions in one message and save them with `seller_profile.py set`).
3. **What Vintoria sells.** 270 services in 12 disciplines — `../vintoria-sales/references/service-catalog.md`. Name a
   service exactly as the catalog does (`192 Shopify Development`), never invent one, and never quote a price, discount or
   date that isn't in the profile (`../vintoria-sales/references/pricing-policy.md`): use `[PRICE TO CONFIRM]`.
4. **The prospect.** Everything about one prospect lives in `prospects/<slug>/` — slug = their domain (`acme.com`), or
   `name@platform` (`anita-desai@linkedin`) without a website. `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py find <email|name|domain>`
   finds it. Never use one prospect's facts for another.
5. **The pipeline.** `~/.ai-sales-team/pipeline.db` is shared by every agent and session. Before you finish, record what
   you learned (stage, note, next action, attached file) — the next agent only sees what the pipeline shows.
6. **Web access.** Your agent's own fetch/search first; otherwise `python <SCRIPTS>/research/fetch_page.py <url> ...` and
   `python <SCRIPTS>/research/web_search.py "<query>"`; with `APIFY_TOKEN`, `python <SCRIPTS>/leads/lead_sources.py`. If you
   can't fetch something, write "Not found".
7. **Facts only.** Every fact traces to a script output, a page saved with `fetch_page.py --save`, the prospect's own
   words, the catalog or the profile — never to memory or to a document you wrote yourself. One invented client, number or
   promise in a cold email destroys trust and can break consumer-protection law. Unknown = "Not found"; a guessed email =
   `pattern-guessed — verify before sending`; missing proof = `[ADD REAL CUSTOMER RESULT]`. Never say something was sent,
   booked, verified or scanned unless a script's output says so. Guards: `../vintoria-sales/references/truth-rules.md`.

## Pick the stage

| Stage | When | Output in `prospects/<slug>/` |
|---|---|---|
| **Prep** | a meeting is booked | `MEETING-PREP.md` |
| **Discovery** | the call is happening / just happened | `MEETING-NOTES.md` + qualification score |
| **Proposal** | discovery scored A (80+), or they asked for one | `CLIENT-PROPOSAL.md` |
| **Objections** | an objection came up (any stage) | answers in the reply, or `OBJECTION-PLAYBOOK.md` |
| **Negotiate & sign** | they've seen the proposal / said yes verbally | terms summary, mutual action plan, handoff checklist |
| **Expand** | won and delivering | upsell map, referral and review asks |

## Prep
1. Load what is known: `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py show <slug>`, `python <SCRIPTS>/replies/meeting.py list`, every file in `prospects/<slug>/` (dossier, post quote, audit,
   research, contacts, outreach, replies). No dossier → run the deep scan (`vintoria-prospect-research`) first.
2. Build the brief (`references/prep-playbook.md`, `references/prep-output-format.md`, `references/template-meeting-prep.md`):
   a one-page cheat sheet, their original request in their own words, the audit facts, the conversation so far,
   qualification status (budget, timeline, decision maker, scope — unknowns marked), 1-3 catalog services that fit with
   the signal beside each, talking points, 10 discovery questions aimed at the unknowns, objections, landmines, and the
   exact ask for the close ("Shall I send a written scope with 3 options by Friday?").
3. A **3-minute screen-share plan**: their site on a phone view, the top 2 audit facts, one competitor doing it better —
   then ask, don't pitch.
4. `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach/claim_check.py --draft prospects/<slug>/MEETING-PREP.md --prospect <slug>` — talking points built on an invented fact are
   worse than none. Attach it: `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py attach <slug> prospects/<slug>/MEETING-PREP.md --kind meeting-prep`.

## Discovery
Run the call with `references/discovery-call.md`: 30- or 45-minute agenda, one question at a time from the banks (core
first, then the discipline bank — AI, automation, web/SaaS, e-commerce, SEO/growth), verbatim quotes, red-flag tests.
Score after the call: problem 20, impact 15, authority 20, timeline 15, budget signal 10, service fit 10, risk 10.
**A 80-100** proposal · **B 65-79** paid discovery or a bounded pilot · **C 50-64** nurture with a trigger · **D < 50**
disqualify politely. Hard no-go whatever the score: no route to the economic buyer within 30 days, need outside the 270
services, regulated data with no compliance owner, free build or guaranteed result demanded. Write `MEETING-NOTES.md`
within 2 hours, then `python <SCRIPTS>/replies/meeting.py outcome <id> held --notes "<budget, scope, decision date>"`,
`python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py score <slug> <score>` and `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py stage <slug> qualified --reason "<band + reason>"` (or `nurture` / `disqualified`).

## Proposal
1. **Inputs** (`references/proposal-playbook.md`): the buyer's needs in their words (post, replies, `MEETING-NOTES.md`),
   and from the profile only: prices, timelines, process, team, case studies. Missing → `[PRICE TO CONFIRM]` /
   `[TO CONFIRM]` — never a guess, never "agencies usually charge".
2. **Write** with `references/template-proposal-template.md` and `references/proposal-output-format.md`: their problem with
   evidence; scope where every line cites a catalog service ID + name with inclusions and **exclusions**; three options
   (core / recommended / expanded) that differ in scope, never in discount; phases with dependencies and acceptance
   criteria (working days, or `[TO CONFIRM]`); assumptions, buyer responsibilities, change-request path; payment terms,
   validity (14 days) and acceptance ("reply 'approved Option B'"). Price in their currency (INR, USD, GBP, AUD) only from
   the profile. Tax on exported services is for the accountant — don't state it.
3. Score it against the proposal bar in `../vintoria-sales/references/quality-bar.md` (floor 80) and claim-check it.
4. Record: `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py stage <slug> proposal --reason "proposal sent <date>"`, `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py value <slug> <recommended option price> --currency <cur>`
   (only a real price), attach the file, `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py next <slug> "Walkthrough offer" --due <date+2>`; the post-proposal follow-ups
   are in `vintoria-outreach`.

## Objections
Exact words for calls, emails and chat (`references/objections-playbook.md`, `references/objections-output-format.md`,
`references/template-objection-playbook.md`): the frameworks, 15 universal objections, industry and competitor
objections, prevention. Price objections change scope or phasing, never the price (`../vintoria-sales/references/pricing-policy.md`).
For overseas buyers include the offshore-agency worries — time zones (overlap hours, response time), communication and
quality (named owner, weekly calls), "burned by an offshore team" (milestone payments, code ownership, references), IP and
contracts, how to pay. For Indian SMBs: "a freelancer will do it cheaper", "my nephew can build it", "we tried an agency
before", "guarantee page 1?" (never promise rankings). Map each to the `reply_triage.py` categories so replies get the
right answer. Proof points must be real — otherwise `[ADD REAL CUSTOMER RESULT]`.

## Negotiate & sign
Follow `references/negotiation-and-handoff.md`: capture their decision criteria, build a mutual action plan with dates
both sides own, sequence concessions that change only scope, phasing or start date, prepare procurement / legal /
security answers, check contract terms against the catalog IDs in scope, rescue a buyer who goes quiet after a yes, and
hand off to delivery with a checklist. `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py stage <slug> won` when signed — or `lost --reason "<why>"` (the reason
feeds the weekly review).

## Expand
After the win, `references/expand-and-referrals.md`: the natural follow-on services from the offer lane (e.g. a website → `212 Search Engine Optimization` or
`214 Local SEO`, then an automation service from discipline 08), timing for the upsell, and the review and referral asks — only when
the client got a real result.

## Before anything is sent

- **Claim check** every message: `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach/claim_check.py --draft <file> --prospect <slug> [--evidence-text "<their post>"]`.
  Rewrite or delete each unsupported sentence and fill every `[PLACEHOLDER]`. Drafts never count as evidence;
  claims about the seller are checked against the real proof in the profile; a hedged consequence ("so phone
  visitors may…") is fine, an unproven number is not.
- **Compliance:** follow `references/compliance.md` — opt-out line, postal address (US), consent rules (UK/EU/CA/AU/NZ).
- **Approval:** show the user the exact final text. Nothing goes out without their OK (a sequence is approved once,
  as a whole).
- **Quality bar:** score it with `../vintoria-sales/references/quality-bar.md` (emails: 80 to show, 85+ is premium).
- **Language:** plain English by default — US spelling for US/CA, UK spelling for UK/IE/AU/NZ, clear Indian English for
  India (no "do the needful", "revert", "kindly"). If an Indian prospect wrote in Hindi or Hinglish, answer in the same
  register, still short and professional. Prices only in their currency and only from the profile.
- **Delivery:** `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach/outreach.py doctor` tells you the mode — `autopilot` (queue the approved sequence with
  `outreach.py approve`; it sends each email on its day and stops on any reply), `send-one-by-one` (`outreach.py send`,
  dry run first, then `--send`), or `manual` (the user sends; you `outreach.py log` it so follow-ups get scheduled).
  Never say something was sent unless a script returned `"sent": true` or the user told you so.
- **No booking link** (Calendly, Cal.com, `BOOKING_URL`) until they've replied with interest — a calendar link in a
  cold email reads as presumptuous and lowers replies; `outreach.py` refuses it.
- Never post, comment or DM on social platforms yourself — give the user the text (or a WhatsApp click-to-chat link).
