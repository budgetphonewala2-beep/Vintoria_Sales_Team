# Proof bank — collect real results, reviews and referrals after every project

Every skill refuses to invent a client or a result, so without real proof the messages stay weaker than they could be
(`[ADD REAL CUSTOMER RESULT]`). The proof bank fixes that honestly: after each project the Closer collects what really
happened, with permission, into `PROOF-BANK.md`. Outreach, proposals and LinkedIn posts then use the closest match.

## When to ask (the Closer schedules these in `PIPELINE.md`)

| When | Ask for | Message (adapt; WhatsApp or email, their language) |
|---|---|---|
| Launch day | A one-line reaction | "It's live — how does it feel seeing it up? Anything you'd like changed in the first week?" |
| Launch + 7 days | A testimonial + permission to name them | "Would you be happy to share 2-3 lines on working with us? And is it OK if we mention <business name> as a client?" |
| Launch + 30 days | The result, with a number they can see | "Has anything changed since launch — bookings, orders, enquiries, time saved? Even a rough number helps." |
| Launch + 30 days | A Google review (only if the profile has a Google Business link) | "If you're happy with the work, a short Google review would mean a lot: <link from profile>." |
| Launch + 45 days | A referral | "Do you know one other owner who's struggling with <the problem we solved>? Happy to help them the same way." |

Ask each thing once, and once more at most after 7 days. "No" is fine — note it and never ask again.

## `PROOF-BANK.md` (one block per proof)

```markdown
## P-007 · Physio clinic, Pune, IN · 028 Booking Platform Development · launched 2026-10-02
Permission: name yes | name no (anonymous only) | quote yes | numbers yes — given by <name> on <date> via <channel>
Problem (their words): "patients call to book and we miss calls in the evening"
What we built: online booking with WhatsApp confirmations, 5 pages, Google Business Profile linked
Result (their number, their source): "about 40 online bookings in the first month" — from the client, 2026-11-03 (WhatsApp)
Quote: "<exact words>" — <name, role> (only if quote = yes)
Links: <live site> · <Google review link>
Use for: clinics, healthcare, booking, India
```

## Rules for using proof

- Use only entries whose **permission** covers the use: name only if `name yes`; otherwise "a physiotherapy clinic in
  Pune". Quotes only if `quote yes`. Numbers only if `numbers yes`, and exactly as the client said them — never rounded up,
  never turned into a percentage you calculated.
- Results are the client's words with their date — say "the clinic told us about 40 bookings came online in the first
  month", not "we increase bookings by 40".
- Pick the **closest** proof: same niche > same service > same country > anything else. No close match → don't force one.
- Entries in `PROOF-BANK.md` count as profile facts for `../../vintoria-sales/references/truth-rules.md`. Anything not
  in it (or in `SELLER-PROFILE.md`) is still not a fact.
- Never write a testimonial for a client, never "improve" their words beyond fixing a typo, never use a logo without
  permission.

## Where proof is used
- Reddit comments: one short line when it's the same niche ("we built this for a physio clinic in Pune last month").
- Email 3 of the sequence (`../../vintoria-outreach/references/sequence-playbook.md`), proposals (case study section),
  WhatsApp after the qualifying questions, LinkedIn "how we did it" posts (`vintoria-authority`).
- Referrals → a new `PIPELINE.md` row (`source` = `referral:<P-id>`, `channel` email or WhatsApp), first message names who
  referred them, only with that client's OK.
