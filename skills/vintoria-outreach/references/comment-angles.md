# Comment angles — useful tips by service, and openings that never repeat

A comment wins when its tip is something the buyer can use today. This bank gives correct, general tips per lane so the
Writer never has to invent advice, and opening shapes so 200 comments a day don't read like one template. Always adapt the
tip to **their** post (their niche, city, platform, problem); never paste a tip that doesn't fit what they asked.

## Opening shapes (rotate; log which one you used in `RUN-LOG.md`)

| # | Shape | Example opening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name the real goal | "For a clinic site, the thing that actually brings patients is online booking, not the design." |
| 2 | The question to ask any developer | "Whoever you hire, ask them how they'll handle <X> — it's where most <niche> sites go wrong." |
| 3 | The common mistake | "The usual mistake with <their project> is <mistake>; worth avoiding from day one." |
| 4 | Decide this first | "Before getting quotes, decide <choice> — it changes the price and timeline more than anything else." |
| 5 | What to prepare | "To get accurate quotes fast, have <3 things> ready — developers can then quote a fixed scope." |
| 6 | Their detail, restated | "Moving a <n>-product store off Wix is very doable — the part to plan is <X>." |
| 7 | Quick diagnosis | "If the store is slow mostly on mobile, it's usually <cause> rather than the hosting." |
| 8 | Phase it | "You don't need everything at once: launch <core> first, add <extra> once it's earning." |

Never open with "We", "I", "Hey there!", "Great question", or the same shape twice in a row in one subreddit.

## Tip bank by lane (pick one or two; make them about their case)

**Websites** — 033 High-Performance Website Development, 028 Booking Platform Development
- Local businesses: the site should link to and match the Google Business Profile (name, address, phone, hours) — that
  consistency helps local search.
- Most visitors for local services arrive on a phone; test the booking or contact step on a phone before launch.
- Ask who owns the domain, hosting and code after launch — it should be the business, not the developer.
- A booking flow needs: services, durations, staff, buffer times and reminders decided before design starts.
- 4-6 focused pages (services, about/team, pricing or packages if public, booking/contact, location) usually beat 20 thin ones.

**Shopify / e-commerce** — 192 Shopify Development
- Slow Shopify stores are most often caused by too many apps and very large images, not the theme itself — check those first.
- Before migrating from another platform, export products, customers and orders and plan URL redirects so search
  rankings aren't lost.
- Payment gateways, shipping rates and taxes per country should be decided before the build, especially when selling
  from India to the US / UK.

**Mobile apps and MVPs** — 066 Mobile MVP Development, 063 Cross-Platform App Development
- Write the 3-5 actions a user must be able to do in version 1; everything else goes to version 2 — that's what keeps an
  MVP fast and affordable.
- One cross-platform codebase (Flutter or React Native) usually covers iOS and Android for a first version.
- Plan the admin side (how you'll manage users, content, orders) — it's often forgotten in quotes.

**AI chatbots and AI agents** — 123 AI Chatbot Development
- A good business chatbot answers from your own content (FAQs, prices, policies) and hands over to a human when unsure.
- Start with the 20 questions customers ask most; measure how many the bot answers correctly before adding features.
- Decide the channel first: website widget, WhatsApp or Instagram — the build differs.

**Automation and CRM** — 147 Workflow Automation, 148 CRM Automation
- Map the process step by step (who does what, in which tool) before choosing Zapier, Make, n8n or custom code.
- Automate the step that happens most often and costs the most time first; one solid workflow beats ten fragile ones.
- Keep one source of truth for customer data (a CRM or a sheet), or automations start fighting each other.

**SEO and local SEO** — 212 Search Engine Optimization, 214 Local SEO
- For local businesses, the Google Business Profile (categories, photos, reviews, posts) often matters more than the website.
- One page per main service and per area served usually ranks better than one page listing everything.
- Be careful with anyone promising guaranteed #1 rankings — no one can guarantee positions on Google.

**Design and branding** — 104 Brand Identity, 105 Logo Design
- Brief the designer with 3 competitor sites you like and dislike, and why — it saves rounds of revisions.
- Ask for the logo in vector format (SVG / AI / PDF) plus versions for dark and light backgrounds.

## Rules
- One or two tips per comment, never a list of five.
- No tip you can't stand behind; no numbers ("loads 3x faster") unless the source is in their post or a checked page.
- The tip must help even if they never contact Vintoria — that's what keeps comments up and earns replies.
- Track in `RUN-LOG.md` which shape + tip got replies; the weekly report keeps the winners.
