# LinkedIn — find the email, write from business@vintoria.org

On LinkedIn the buyer's post and profile usually give you everything: who they are, their company, and often an email.
The main path is a short, personal email from `business@vintoria.org`; a professional comment or connection note is
the fallback. Contact details: `../../vintoria-sales/references/vintoria-contact.md`.

## Find the email (stop at the first that works)

1. **The post itself** — many buyers write "email me at…" or "send proposals to…". Status `published`.
2. **Their profile → Contact info** (visible to the logged-in account, fully for 1st-degree connections). Status `published`.
3. **Their company website** (linked on the profile): contact, about and team pages, the footer. A personal or role
   address there (`founder@`, `hello@`) → `published`.
4. **Nothing found** → don't guess a pattern for cold email. Use the fallback below.

Record the email, where you found it and the status in `PIPELINE.md` and `DECISION-MAKERS.md`.

## The email (main path)

Follow `first-email-guide.md` and the 5-email cadence in the skill: line 1 is their post in their words ("Saw your post
about rebuilding your clinic's booking system"), one sourced observation from their site or post, one sentence on what
Vintoria does about it with one catalog service, a soft question, and the email sign-off from `../../vintoria-sales/references/vintoria-contact.md`
(it includes the WhatsApp link and the opt-out line). Subject 2-5 words, about them ("your clinic booking site").

## Fallback — no email

- **Comment on their post** (40-80 words), the same shape as a Reddit comment but shorter: their need → one useful
  point → "We're Vintoria — we build <service>." → "— <name>, Vintoria · business@vintoria.org". LinkedIn comments with
  links get less reach, so put the email, not the WhatsApp link, in a comment.
- **Connection request with a note** (≤ 300 characters, no link): "Hi <name>, saw your post about <their need>. We build
  exactly this at Vintoria — happy to share a quick plan if useful. — <sender name>". Once they accept, a 2-3 line message
  can carry the WhatsApp link.
- **DM** only to people who invited it ("DM me", "send me your portfolio") or who accepted the connection.

## Limits (keep it human)

LinkedIn's terms don't allow automated activity on accounts, so the bot works at a person's pace: at most **10 comments,
15 connection requests and 20 DMs a day**, spread across the day, never the same text twice. If LinkedIn shows a warning
or a "you're sending too many invitations" limit, stop LinkedIn actions for the day and tell the user.

## Log it

`PIPELINE.md`: `channel` linkedin-email / linkedin-comment / linkedin-note, stage `contacted`, `next_action` and `next_due`
from the cadence; the message in `THREAD.md`; the post URL and what you sent in `HISTORY.md`. Check LinkedIn messages and
the inbox of `business@vintoria.org` daily for replies → `vintoria-reply-book`.
