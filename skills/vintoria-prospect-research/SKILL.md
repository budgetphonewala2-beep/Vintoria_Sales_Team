# Vintoria Prospect Research — everything about one prospect

Outreach is only as good as the research under it. You turn one URL, post or name into facts with sources, a
verified way to reach the decision maker, and the 1-3 Vintoria services the evidence actually justifies.

## Before you start

1. **Scripts.** `<SCRIPTS>` is the engine folder `../vintoria-sales/scripts/` (next to this skill's folder), grouped by
   job: `core/` (pipeline, seller profile), `leads/`, `research/`, `outreach/`, `replies/`, `ops/`. Run them with
   `python` (Windows) or `python3` (macOS/Linux). Each prints one JSON object — evidence to check, not truth.
2. **Vintoria's profile.** Run `python <SCRIPTS>/core/seller_profile.py check`. Queries, emails and prices are built
   from `SELLER-PROFILE.json`; if it isn't `Ready.`, run `vintoria-setup` first (in a hurry: ask only the missing required
   questions in one message and save them with `seller_profile.py set`).
3. **What Vintoria sells.** 270 services in 12 disciplines — `../vintoria-sales/references/service-catalog.md`. Name a
   service exactly as the catalog does (`192 Shopify Development`), never invent one, and never quote a price, discount or
   date that isn't in the profile (`../vintoria-sales/references/pricing-policy.md`): use `[PRICE TO CONFIRM]`.
4. **The prospect.** Everything about one prospect lives in `prospects/<slug>/` — slug = their domain (`acme.com`), or
   `name@platform` (`anita-desai@linkedin`) without a website. `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py find <email|name|domain>`
   finds it. Never use one prospect's facts for another.
5. **The pipeline.** `~/.ai-sales-team/pipeline.db` is shared by every agent and session. Before you finish, record what
   you learned (stage, note, next action, attached file) — the next agent only sees what the pipeline shows.
6. **Web access.** Your agent's own fetch/search first; otherwise `python <SCRIPTS>/research/fetch_page.py <url> ...` and
   `python <SCRIPTS>/research/web_search.py "<query>"`; with `APIFY_TOKEN`, `python <SCRIPTS>/leads/lead_sources.py`. If you
   can't fetch something, write "Not found".
7. **Facts only.** Every fact traces to a script output, a page saved with `fetch_page.py --save`, the prospect's own
   words, the catalog or the profile — never to memory or to a document you wrote yourself. One invented client, number or
   promise in a cold email destroys trust and can break consumer-protection law. Unknown = "Not found"; a guessed email =
   `pattern-guessed — verify before sending`; missing proof = `[ADD REAL CUSTOMER RESULT]`. Never say something was sent,
   booked, verified or scanned unless a script's output says so. Guards: `../vintoria-sales/references/truth-rules.md`.

## Pick the mode

| The user wants | Mode | Output in `prospects/<slug>/` |
|---|---|---|
| "Look into this lead" (default for any new prospect) | **Deep scan** | `DOSSIER.json` + `DOSSIER.md` |
| A hook / "what's wrong with their site" | **Site audit** | `SITE-AUDIT.json` + `SITE-AUDIT.md` |
| Background on the company | **Company research** | `COMPANY-RESEARCH.md` |
| Who to contact + their email | **Contacts** | `DECISION-MAKERS.md` |
| Is it worth our time | **Qualify** | `LEAD-QUALIFICATION.md` |
| What they use today / who their agency is | **Competitors** | `COMPETITIVE-INTEL.md` |
| A full scored audit with a first email | **Full analysis** | `PROSPECT-ANALYSIS.md` |

Always start from the deep scan when there is no `DOSSIER.json`; the other modes build on it.

## Deep scan
`python <SCRIPTS>/research/prospect_scan.py <url> [--name "<company>"] [--post-text "<their post>"] [--city <city>]` — audit + PageSpeed, company, people,
best email (skipped if known), web research (reviews, news, hiring, complaints, ads, competitors) and ranked hooks. From
a buyer post use `python <SCRIPTS>/leads/post_lead.py ...` instead (see `vintoria-lead-hunt` step 6). Then add **Fit**: for each top hook name the
catalog service it justifies (no viewport + slow → `033 High-Performance Website Development`; no booking →
`028 Booking Platform Development`; manual enquiry handling → an automation service from discipline 08). The dossier must
reach 70 on the dossier score in `../vintoria-sales/references/quality-bar.md` before outreach.

## Site audit
`python <SCRIPTS>/research/site_audit.py --url <url> --save prospects/<slug>/SITE-AUDIT.json [--pagespeed] [--no-links]` (robots.txt respected). Write
`SITE-AUDIT.md` (URL, date, every `statement` + `evidence`) and pick the **top 3 observations** for this business with
`references/fact-to-hook.md` — the fact exactly as measured, any consequence hedged ("so phone visitors may…"), never a
guessed number. Local businesses: compare with the top nearby rivals (`python <SCRIPTS>/leads/lead_sources.py maps --search "<category> in <area>" --max 10`).
Never claim certificate or security problems from a TLS error on your side.

## Company research
Fill the 8 dimensions of `references/research-playbook.md`, adapted to the business (owner, locations, reviews, site and
marketing for an SMB; product and pricing for SaaS; catalogue and channels for e-commerce). Save every page you rely on:
`python <SCRIPTS>/research/fetch_page.py <url> --save prospects/<slug>/sources` (snippets are leads, not facts). End `COMPANY-RESEARCH.md`
(`references/research-output-format.md`) with **Why now** and the **3 best hooks**, then
`python <SCRIPTS>/outreach/claim_check.py --draft prospects/<slug>/COMPANY-RESEARCH.md --prospect <slug>` and fix every unsupported line.

## Contacts
Start from the dossier's `people` / `decision_maker`; otherwise `python <SCRIPTS>/research/contact_finder.py --url <url>`,
the team page and `site:linkedin.com/in "<company>" founder OR owner OR director`. Classify buying roles
(`references/contacts-playbook.md`). Emails for the top 1-3: `python <SCRIPTS>/research/find_email.py --domain <domain> --first <first> --last <last>` —
post/site first, then finders, then verifiers (`python <SCRIPTS>/research/find_email.py --providers` shows what is set); an address from a database:
`python <SCRIPTS>/research/find_email.py --email <address>` (verify only). Use the status exactly as returned; only `verified` is safe for cold email, and
typing `--email-status verified` without a verification on record gets downgraded. Best channel: verified email → email;
a published business phone → WhatsApp click-to-chat (`python <SCRIPTS>/outreach/outreach.py whatsapp`); only a social profile → the user replies or DMs.
Write `DECISION-MAKERS.md` (`references/contacts-output-format.md`) and save each person:
`python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py contact <slug> --name "<name>" --title "<title>" --role "<buying role>" --email <email> --email-status <status> --source <source> --linkedin <profile url>`.

## Qualify
BANT (25 each) and MEDDIC with evidence or "Unknown" (`references/qualify-playbook.md`). Cross-check:
`python <SCRIPTS>/leads/lead_scorer.py prospects/<slug>/_bant_signals.json` (format: `python <SCRIPTS>/leads/lead_scorer.py --example`), or `python <SCRIPTS>/leads/lead_scorer.py --mode agency <file>` for a
public ask; explain any gap over 15 points. Service-buyer red flags — wants free samples or spec work, "just exploring",
equity only, budget far below scope, many agencies already in the comments — two or more → `nurture` or `disqualified`.
Write `LEAD-QUALIFICATION.md` (`references/qualify-output-format.md`) with 3-5 discovery questions for the gaps, then
`python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py stage <slug> qualified --reason "<grade + reason>"` (or `nurture` / `disqualified`).

## Competitors
Detect today's site platform, footer credits ("Designed by…"), job posts, case studies, reviews and the `competitors` /
`ads` research sections; for local businesses their rivals on Maps. One honest battle card per incumbent, switching
cost, landmine questions (`references/competitors-playbook.md`) → `COMPETITIVE-INTEL.md`
(`references/competitors-output-format.md`). Never disparage a competitor with a claim you can't source.

## Full analysis
Deep scan → the five roles in order — `references/role-company.md`, `role-contacts.md`, `role-opportunity.md`,
`role-competitive.md`, then `role-strategy.md` (it needs the other four; agents with subagents may run the first four in
parallel). Prospect Score = Company Fit 25% + Contact Access 20% + Opportunity 20% + Competitive 15% + Outreach Readiness
20% (missing role = 50, lower confidence). Write `PROSPECT-ANALYSIS.md` (`references/analysis-playbook.md`,
`references/analysis-output-format.md`) including a claim-checked first email, then
`python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py score <slug> <score> --breakdown '{"company_fit": .., "contact_access": .., "opportunity": .., "competitive": .., "outreach_readiness": ..}'`.

## Record (every mode)
`python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py add --url <url> --name "<company>" --stage researched`, `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py attach <slug> prospects/<slug>/<FILE> --kind <kind>`,
`python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py note <slug> "<top hooks + services>"`, `python <SCRIPTS>/core/pipeline.py next <slug> "<next action>" --due <YYYY-MM-DD>`. Finish with: score
+ grade, top 3 hooks with their catalog services, contact status, and the next skill (usually `vintoria-outreach`).

## Before anything is sent

- **Claim check** every message: `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach/claim_check.py --draft <file> --prospect <slug> [--evidence-text "<their post>"]`.
  Rewrite or delete each unsupported sentence and fill every `[PLACEHOLDER]`. Drafts never count as evidence;
  claims about the seller are checked against the real proof in the profile; a hedged consequence ("so phone
  visitors may…") is fine, an unproven number is not.
- **Compliance:** follow `references/compliance.md` — opt-out line, postal address (US), consent rules (UK/EU/CA/AU/NZ).
- **Approval:** show the user the exact final text. Nothing goes out without their OK (a sequence is approved once,
  as a whole).
- **Quality bar:** score it with `../vintoria-sales/references/quality-bar.md` (emails: 80 to show, 85+ is premium).
- **Language:** plain English by default — US spelling for US/CA, UK spelling for UK/IE/AU/NZ, clear Indian English for
  India (no "do the needful", "revert", "kindly"). If an Indian prospect wrote in Hindi or Hinglish, answer in the same
  register, still short and professional. Prices only in their currency and only from the profile.
- **Delivery:** `python <SCRIPTS>/outreach/outreach.py doctor` tells you the mode — `autopilot` (queue the approved sequence with
  `outreach.py approve`; it sends each email on its day and stops on any reply), `send-one-by-one` (`outreach.py send`,
  dry run first, then `--send`), or `manual` (the user sends; you `outreach.py log` it so follow-ups get scheduled).
  Never say something was sent unless a script returned `"sent": true` or the user told you so.
- **No booking link** (Calendly, Cal.com, `BOOKING_URL`) until they've replied with interest — a calendar link in a
  cold email reads as presumptuous and lowers replies; `outreach.py` refuses it.
- Never post, comment or DM on social platforms yourself — give the user the text (or a WhatsApp click-to-chat link).
