# WhatsApp — where Vintoria's deals are won

Every Reddit comment and most emails end with Vintoria's WhatsApp link, so the buyers who are most interested arrive here
first. A person who taps the link is hot for **minutes**, not days: answer fast, sound like a person, qualify gently,
and move to a call. The Closer owns WhatsApp (`../../vintoria-sales/references/team.md`); the business phone sends the
messages (the owner, or a bot with access to WhatsApp Web / WhatsApp Business).

## Speed

| They wrote | Answer within |
|---|---|
| 09:00-21:00 in **their** time zone | 5 minutes (target), 15 minutes at most |
| Outside those hours | the first thing in their morning; an instant one-line reply is fine if the phone is set up for it |

A slow first answer loses more deals than a weak one. Post an `ALERT` on the team board the moment a WhatsApp message arrives.

## Message 1 — recognise them

Find them first: the pre-filled text ("I saw your reply on my Reddit post about…"), their number's country code, or their
name in `PIPELINE.md` / `THREAD.md`. Then:

> Hi <name>, this is Pratik Patil from Vintoria — thanks for reaching out about <their request, in their words>.
> Quick question so I point you the right way: <the single most useful qualifying question>?

Unknown sender, no context → "Hi, this is Pratik Patil from Vintoria — thanks for messaging. What are you looking to
build or fix?"

## Qualify in 3-5 short messages (never a questionnaire)

Ask one question at a time, in this order, skipping anything they already said:

| # | Learn | English | Hinglish (only if they wrote Hinglish) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What exactly | "What should the site / app do for you — bookings, orders, enquiries?" | "Website se aapko kya chahiye — bookings, orders ya enquiries?" |
| 2 | What exists | "Do you have a site or app today, or is this from scratch?" | "Abhi koi website/app hai, ya naya banana hai?" |
| 3 | When | "Is there a date you'd like it live by?" | "Kab tak live chahiye?" |
| 4 | Budget | "Do you have a budget range in mind? It helps me suggest the right scope." | "Budget ka koi range socha hai? Usse sahi scope suggest kar paunga." |
| 5 | Who decides | "Is it just you deciding, or should anyone else join the call?" | "Decision aap hi loge, ya call pe koi aur bhi hoga?" |

Write each answer into `PIPELINE.md` / `HISTORY.md` as you go (scope, timeline, budget, decision maker).

## Show, then move to a call

- Send **one or two** relevant examples — only real portfolio links or results from `SELLER-PROFILE.md`, closest to their
  niche or platform. None yet → say what we would build and how, and flag `[ADD REAL CUSTOMER RESULT]` to the owner.
- Then the call: "Easiest is a 15-minute call — I'll walk you through how we'd do it and what it would cost. Does
  <slot 1> or <slot 2> work?" (two slots in their time zone, `../SKILL.md` → Book). A WhatsApp voice / video call is fine
  if they prefer it.

## "Kitna lagega?" / "How much?"

`../../vintoria-sales/references/pricing-policy.md`. A price only if `SELLER-PROFILE.md` has it, in their currency.
Otherwise: "It depends on <the 1-2 things that change the price for their project> — tell me <the missing answer> and I'll
send you a written scope with 2-3 options today." Never a number from memory, never "starting at" unless the profile says it.

## Style on WhatsApp

- Short messages (1-3 lines), one question per message, no walls of text, no PDFs before they ask.
- Warm and professional: no emojis beyond an occasional 🙂 if they use them first, no "dear sir", no "kindly revert".
- Their language: English, Hindi or Hinglish — match them, stay polite (aap, not tum).
- Voice notes: listen fully, answer in text unless they prefer voice.
- Never send the same message twice, never send broadcast / bulk messages, never add people to groups.

## Follow-ups on WhatsApp

| They went quiet after | Follow-up |
|---|---|
| Your question | Next day: one line that makes answering easy ("Even a rough idea of the pages you need is enough to start.") |
| The examples / slots | 2 days later: one new useful thing (an idea for their site, a fix) + "Shall we keep <slot> or pick another time?" |
| The proposal | Day 2, day 5, day 10 per `../../vintoria-close/SKILL.md`; then `nurture` |

At most 3 follow-ups without an answer, then stop and mark `nurture`. "Not now" / "no" → one gracious line, stop.
"Stop" or "don't message me" → `DO-NOT-CONTACT.md` at once.

## Hand-offs

- A call is booked → `PIPELINE.md` stage `meeting`, `HANDOFF` on the board to the Closer's prep (`vintoria-close`).
- They want it in writing → proposal (`vintoria-close`, Proposal), sent as a PDF or link, with a WhatsApp summary of
  the 3 options in 4 lines.
- Anything about money already paid, a complaint, or a discount not in the profile → `ESCALATE` to the owner.
