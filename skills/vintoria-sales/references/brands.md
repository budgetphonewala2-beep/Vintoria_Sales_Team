## Honest identity (non-negotiable)

- Each email says truthfully who it is from: the real sender name, the real brand, its real postal address and a working
  opt-out. Never a made-up person, company or address.
- Radian's emails never mention Vintoria and Vintoria's never mention Radian — no fake comparisons, no "we saw another
  agency contacted you", no pretending to be a rival, no reviews of one brand written by the other.
- If a prospect asks whether the two are connected, the answer is honest ("Yes, both are run by the same team — happy to
  keep it to one of us if you prefer."), then that prospect continues with **one** brand only.

- **Proof never crosses brands.** Vintoria's emails never cite Radian's clients, testimonials or case studies, and Radian's
  never cite Vintoria's.

## One person, one conversation (shared rules)

1. **Suppression is shared.** A "stop", unsubscribe, complaint or bounce on either brand → `DO-NOT-CONTACT.md` for
   **both** brands, at once.
2. **A reply to either brand stops both sequences.** The brand they answered continues the conversation
   (`vintoria-reply-book`); the other brand never writes to them again.
3. **Never two emails to one person on the same day**, and at most **4 emails in any 7 days across both brands**. On a
   clash, the later email moves one day.
4. **Radian only when its angle is real.** It needs its own hook — a marketing fact you actually checked (Google Business
   Profile incomplete, no reviews answered, no meta descriptions, ads running to a slow page). No real marketing hook → no
   Radian sequence; Vintoria's runs alone.
5. Cold email only to `verified` / `published` addresses, per country rules in `compliance.md`.

## Making Radian's email more personal (it arrives second, so it must be better)

- Spend 5 more minutes: their Google Business Profile (rating, review count, unanswered reviews), their site's title and
  meta description, whether they run Meta or Google ads (ads libraries), their last 3 LinkedIn posts.
- Open with the most specific of those facts, in their words where possible; one idea; a soft question. Under 110 words.
- Never reuse a fact or sentence from Vintoria's email to the same person.

## Calendar for one lead (both brands, all checks passing)

Each brand follows up 3, 7, 14 and 28 days after **its own** first email, so the two brands always stay one day apart:

| Day | Brand | Email |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Vintoria | 1 — their post + build angle |
| 1 | Radian | 1 — marketing angle, deeper research |
| 3 | Vintoria | 2 |
| 4 | Radian | 2 |
| 7 | Vintoria | 3 |
| 8 | Radian | 3 |
| 14 | Vintoria | 4 |
| 15 | Radian | 4 |
| 28 | Vintoria | 5 — breakup |
| 29 | Radian | 5 — breakup |

This calendar never has two emails on one day or more than 4 in any 7 days. Any reply, opt-out or bounce ends both rows.

**Weekends and clashes:** send Vintoria's email 1 only Monday to Thursday in their time zone, so Radian's lands the next
working day. Any later email that falls on their weekend moves to their next working day; if that puts two emails on the
same day, Radian's moves one more working day. Both brands' emails count toward the same 4-in-7-days cap.

**Sending domain:** Radian's public site and email are on radianmarketing.com; outreach goes from radianmarketing.site.
Make radianmarketing.site redirect to radianmarketing.com and put "Reply-To" on the outreach address so replies are
never lost. Until the owner confirms the redirect, the Radian sequence stays in `review` mode.

**Deliverability:** a new domain (radianmarketing.site) needs SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up and a warm-up — start with
10-15 emails a day and add about 5 a week while bounces stay under 2 %. Never send both brands from the same mailbox.
