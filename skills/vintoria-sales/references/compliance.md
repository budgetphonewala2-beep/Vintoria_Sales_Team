# Outreach compliance (guidance, not legal advice)

Every message must be legal to send. When a market's rule is unclear, ask the user before sending.

## Every market

- Truthful sender (real name, Vintoria, a reply address that is read) and honest subject lines — no fake "Re:"/"Fwd:".
- A working opt-out in every cold email: "Not relevant? Reply 'stop' and I won't contact you again."
- Honour opt-outs at once and forever: add the address (and on request the domain) to `DO-NOT-CONTACT.md` and never
  contact that person again on any channel. Check `DO-NOT-CONTACT.md` before every message.
- Contacts come from the prospect's own site or post, a public business profile, or a database the user is licensed to
  use — never a bought or scraped list.
- Only mention a mutual connection the user confirmed is real and agreed to be named.
- "How did you get my email?" gets an honest answer (their post, their site, a database) and an offer to remove them.

## Market rules

| Market | Rule | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| United States | CAN-SPAM | Accurate headers and subject, the **physical postal address** in every commercial email, opt-out honoured within 10 business days |
| United Kingdom | PECR + UK GDPR | B2B email to corporate addresses is allowed with identification + opt-out; sole traders and partnerships need prior consent |
| EU | GDPR + ePrivacy | Documented legitimate interest and role relevance; Germany and some others effectively need prior consent — flag them |
| Canada | CASL | Consent or a documented business relationship; identify yourself and your address — flag Canadian prospects |
| Australia | Spam Act 2003 | Consent — inferred only when the address is conspicuously published for business and your message fits their role; accurate sender, working unsubscribe |
| New Zealand | UEMA 2007 | Similar consent rule; sender details and unsubscribe |
| India | DPDP Act 2023 + TRAI | Business contact details only, say who you are and why you're writing, honour a withdrawal at once; no unsolicited commercial calls or SMS to lists; WhatsApp only to a business number they published, one person at a time |

## Channels

- **Email** — one person at a time, never a bulk blast. Plain text, at most one link (none in email 1), no tracking
  pixels, no attachments in the first email, only to `verified` or `published` addresses, from a warmed-up domain with
  SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Start around 5 cold emails a day per inbox and grow slowly.
- **Reddit** (bot comments from Vintoria's logged-in account): only on real buyer posts, only where the subreddit's rules
  allow offers, one comment per post, said openly to be from Vintoria, helpful first, no identical text, at most 8 a day
  and 20+ minutes apart; no vote manipulation, no second accounts. A removed comment or a warning stops Reddit activity
  until the user reviews it. Reddit's rules and each subreddit's rules win over this playbook.
- **LinkedIn** (bot works from the logged-in account): LinkedIn's terms don't allow automation, so keep a person's pace —
  at most 10 comments, 15 connection requests and 20 DMs a day, no identical text; stop for the day at any warning.
  DMs only to people who invited them or accepted a connection.
- **WhatsApp** — Vintoria's link (`https://wa.me/918766033979`) is offered so the buyer messages first. Vintoria
  writes to a prospect's WhatsApp only after they messaged or shared their number; no bulk, no groups.
- **Calls** — only after a reply, a referral, or to a business number published for that purpose.
- At most 4 touches per prospect per week across all channels; a reply on any channel stops every other channel.
- No booking link in cold emails — it reads as presumptuous; send it only after they show interest.

## Stop-loss

If more than 2 % of emails bounce over two weeks, stop and re-check the list. If fewer than 1 % get a reply after 300
emails, the targeting or the offer is wrong — change them, don't send more.

End every outreach output with one line: `Compliance notes:` + the rules that apply to this prospect's country.
